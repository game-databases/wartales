### Grounds

**F1 — ACCEPT.** The receipt is decisive: `#26221B` has zero occurrences in
style.css, and §2.6 (`design/spec-tokens.mdx:163`) is the one token table
with no game-source column — precisely where an unevidenced value could
hide. Every other value in the spec traces to a cited literal, sampled PNG,
or filter string; this one does not, and the spec's whole authority rests on
that traceability. The three fix routes are all acceptable; preferred order:
(a) extract `buttonBg.png` (already on the §0 follow-up list) and sample its
hover frame; failing that, (b) state a documented wash-over-`--bg-2`
derivation formula; last resort (c) substitute an evidenced value.

**F2 — ACCEPT.** Recomputed from scratch within the brief's two-ratio
budget: `#FFF7DB` on `#B63131` = **5.64:1**, white on `#B63131` = 6.05:1 —
matching the reviewer and matching no reading of 6.7. Because §6.2
(`design/spec-tokens.mdx:273`) asserts the number inside the contrast test,
the shipped test would fail against reality; correct both occurrences
(lines 164 and 273) to 5.64:1. Still AA (≥4.5) — design intent survives.

**F3 — ACCEPT.** The §6.2 matrix list verifiably omits `--warning`, and the

computations on the reviewer's 4.29:1 figure; the fix does not depend on it:
adding `--warning` to the matrix makes the test measure the true value, and
constrain-surfaces vs brighten-token then falls out of §2.3 documentation.
