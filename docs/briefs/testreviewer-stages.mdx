## Mission — break the suite

Prove the tests are not complete enough or don't cover all the cases:
1. **AC/T coverage gaps**: walk EVERY acceptance criterion and T-number
   in the spec; name any with no asserting case (or whose assertion
   doesn't actually pin the criterion).
2. **Fixture shortcuts**: cases that pass vacuously (asserting on data
   they just wrote, catching broad exceptions, skipif conditions that
   will never unskip, mini-fixture worlds that diverge from real corpus
   shapes without saying so).
3. **Wrong frozen numbers**: re-measure 5+ pinned numbers independently
   (regeneration protocol or direct counts) and compare against what
   tests assert.
4. **Blindness checks**: mutate-and-expect-red experiments — for ≥6
   representative cases, plant the fault the case claims to catch and
   verify it goes red; for ≥3, plant an ADJACENT fault and check whether
   the suite wrongly stays green (that's a hole).
5. **The RED case**: validate_all KeyError on non-managed kind — is the
   test itself correct and minimal, or does it over-specify the crash?
6. Skip discipline: every skip justified + armed with its unblock.

For each finding: severity BLOCKER/MAJOR/MINOR, file:line, proof,
concrete missing-test sketch. Run pytest freely (temp on D: if C: is
tight).
