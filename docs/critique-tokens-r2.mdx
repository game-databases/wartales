## Verdict: SEND-BACK

Narrower than r1, and for one reason only. The composition rebuild is real
progress — the preview now shows panels instead of swatches, and the tooltip
card is the first thing in this pack I would believe is Wartales-adjacent.
But the r1 re-entry bar was a conjunction — *"panel anatomy, type voice, and
rarity slots must be attributable to Wartales before the palette is even
considered"* — and the **type voice still renders Georgia on the loudest
pixels of the page**, exactly where r1 put the single hardest
illusion-breaker. Worse than silent fallback this time: the type-specimen
panel **prints "flavor serif — IM Fell English" and "UI face — EB Garamond"
over Georgia glyphs**. A specimen that mislabels the face it renders is not
evidence of the serif-first canon; it is a false claim in the render, and it
sits on the page's most-read pixels for the second round running.

### Blind verdict (captions discounted, shots opened after)

- **Panel anatomy — close, two tells.** Band + tucked body + double hairline
  bevel + square corners is the game's grammar, and the tooltip card's
  anatomy (icon, rarity name + star, italic desc, stat rows, gold price row)
  mirrors `shot-02`'s tip family convincingly. The tells: (1) **the quilt is
  on the wrong surface** — the game quilts the panel *frame* (`shot-02`
  inventory border, the `RarityBackground` texture itself); the preview
  quilts the body interior and leaves the frame a 3px hairline. (2) Band
  titles sit left-aligned; every Wartales band title ("Inventory", "Trade",
  "Defence", "Attributes" in shots 02/03) is **centered**.
- **Type voice — fail.** Georgia everywhere. At 25px display size the
  difference between Georgia's modern ball-terminal serif and IM Fell's rough
  17th-century cut is not subtle; against `shot-03`'s Garamond text ramp the
  specimen reads "browser default", which is the exact read r1 failed for.
- **Rarity slots — two of three.** Rare's olive wash + static sheen and
  legendary's violet + gradient ring read correctly and match the sanctioned
  spec-§3 treatment. **Uncommon is invisible, not quiet**: `#151A1E` on the
  quilted well is indistinguishable from common at table distance — I could
  not name the uncommon slots without the caption. In `shot-02` the steel
  wash reads because it is a *frame texture*, not a flat fill one shade off
  the well. The real `RarityBackground.png` frames remain unrendered.

### §5 feel bar

- **Finished-object feel:** the tooltip passes; the inventory panel does not —
  a 4-column, 246px grid floats centered in a full-width quilted body with
  ~400px of empty quilt either side. The game's inventory fills its frame
  edge to edge (`shot-02`). Sparse grid in a wide panel reads "component
  demo", not "holding the object".
- **Empty well honesty: pass.** One quiet same-size rectangle, no caption
  sentence about it — the game's own move.
- **Gold discipline: pass, exemplary.** Gold on exactly the price row and the
  one selected row's ring, nowhere else; no gold body text; the rare sheen
  stays inside the `#FFFFAA` tip family (no invented hue).
- **Hover vocabulary: half.** The gold selection ring is rendered on the
  Armor row — good. The white outline hover exists only as a caption
  ("hover a slot: outline(0.5, white)"). A caption narrating a state the
  render never shows is a summary, and this round grades renders. r1 gap 5
  asked for the hover state *shown*; it still is not.

## Single biggest gap + fix

**Gap: the two faces the whole soul claim rests on have never rendered — and
the preview now asserts them while rendering Georgia.**

**Fix (one step, then re-shoot):** fetch the five woff2 per
[site/public/fonts/README.md](../site/public/fonts/README.md) **and fix the
resolution path** — `tokens.css` declares root-absolute
`url("/fonts/…")`, which under the brief's `file://` shoot resolves to the
drive root and can never load the binaries even once they land. Either serve
`site/public/` over localhost for the shoot, or point the preview's font
sources at a path that resolves from `site/src/styles/`. Then re-shoot this
same composition set. While in there: move the quilt to the panel frame,
center the band titles, widen the slot grid to fill its body, and render the
white-outline hover state statically (a `.is-hover` demo cell, not a
caption).

## What survives (do not re-litigate)

The extracted token set, the palette discipline, the composition concept, the
tooltip anatomy, gold discipline, empty-well honesty, square corners, the
sanctioned legendary/rare border treatments, and the r1 craft fixes are all
in and correct. r3 is not a rebuild: it is **staging the two binary carriers
that already have contracts (fonts, element PNGs), rendering them, and
showing the hover state** — then this composition set gets re-graded against
the same three shots.

**Re-entry bar for r3:** with only the r3 preview on screen and no captions,
IM Fell English must be identifiable in the display samples and EB Garamond
in the ramp (Georgia nowhere), at least one panel must carry the real
9-slice/texture chrome from `design/extracted-ui/`, the uncommon slot must
be nameable at arm's length, and the white hover outline must be visible in
the render itself.
