# Orientation evidence — world-map axis directions (D6 pre-dig)

Fresh data-layer dig, 2026-08-25 (brief
[briefs/dig-orientation.mdx](briefs/dig-orientation.mdx)). Read-only on the
client; no subagents; no commits. Question: which way are +x/+y on screen in
the stored artifact plane (map.pak tile grid x[−8..43], y[0..39]), and what
are `crs.yAxis` / `transform.d` for the served plane?

## Verdict

**Orientation is closed: `yAxis: "north-up"`, `transform.d: −1`, `a: +1`
(no mirror).** In the stored plane, **north = small y (image top), east =
+x (image right)**. A served north-up plane must flip the y axis
(`py = C − y·s`, i.e. `d = −1`) and needs no x change. Confidence **high**
— convergent client-side evidence, threads 2+3 below; no thread contradicts.

Not yet done (unchanged gates): pinning these values into maps.json happens
on a pipeline rerun (registry stage reads this doc); AC9's roundtrip budget
still waits on D5 anchors.

## Thread 2 — hlboot bytecode (`extracted/logic/hl-structure/`)

New scratch tool `output/_dig-map/orientation_disasm2.py`: disassembles the
fork's opcode stream over the already-verified datasets. Fork facts pinned
en route (all verified against known-semantics functions, see log entry):

- field ops (`OField/OGetThis/OSetThis/OSetField`) carry **flattened
  per-type field ordinals** (root ancestor → leaf), not string indices
  (`h2d.Camera.set_x/y/anchorX` → 0/1/6; `$Math.PI` at ordinal 8 through the
  `hl.BaseType→hl.Class→$Math` chain);
- jump targets are signed offsets, destination = `pc + 1 + t`;
- call-family op 28 has a fixed 6-operand layout (left undecoded; not needed).

### 2a. Every CPU-side world→pixel sampler is UNFLIPPED (confidence: high)

All of them share one frame with the tile grid — whatever cardinal binding
the frame has applies to tiles, layers PNGs, FOW and minimap alike:

- `WorldMesh.getFow(x,y)`: `col=(x−mesh.minX)/fowScale`,
  `row=(y−mesh.minY)/fowScale`, byte `fow[row·fowSize+col]` — **y is the
  row index**, no negation.
- `Layers2D.getLayerColor(pixels,x,y)`: `int((x−offsetX)/layerScale)`,
  `int((y−offsetY)/layerScale)` → `Pixels.getPixel(col,row)`; hxd loads PNG
  rows top-down, so **world +y runs down-image** in every layers2D map.
- `MiniMap.getPlayerPosition()`: returns `(me.x, me.y)` (or `fakePosition`)
  — panel space is world xy directly.
- `MiniMap.getInitialPanning()`: `(pos.x·zoom, pos.y·zoom)`; update applies
  `scroll.x/y = −panning.x/y + C` (symmetric camera negation, not an axis
  flip).
- `MiniMap.update`: inner container `camera.rotation = atan2(dy,dx)` toward
  the 3D camera target, **no offset and no negation** — the strategic map is
  *rotated* to the camera heading at runtime; it never flips axes.

### 2b. Compass widget (`ui.comp.Compass`) — decoded, then DISCARDED as tiebreak

`sync` computes a camera yaw `±acos(f·X̂)` (sign from a `fy` comparison) and
places N/E/S/W labels from bearing windows `N(0,π) E(+π/2,−π/2)
W(−π/2,+π/2) S(−π,0)` with K = `Math.PI`. Read literally this binds
North=+Y / East=−X — contradicting thread 3. The literal reading rests on
two coupled sign choices I could not close without deeper dataflow analysis
(the fork's comparison-jump operand order, and which `angleData` field
drives label-x vs alpha across a strip whose interpolation direction depends
on `innerWidth`). Both reversals together would flip the reading to
North=−Y/East=+X, i.e. exactly thread 3's verdict. **Recorded as ambiguous;
not used as evidence.** (The widget is a Skyrim-style heading bar whose
content the game also rotates — consistent with either reading.)

**Client directional lore** (`export_en.xml`, EN strings quoted verbatim):

- *"Where would you like to go: south to Tiltren or **north to Ludern**?"*
  ⇒ Ludern is north of Tiltren.
- *"…when Alazar was still called the **Northern Kingdom**"* + *"Keep your
  **mountains** and your science, wicked king!"* ⇒ Alazar/Drombach is the
  mountainous far north.
- *"a Belerian ship has washed up on the **southern coast of Tiltren**"*
  ⇒ sea lies south of Tiltren; the archipelago sits off it.

**Measurement** (client imagery, same plane as the tiles):

- Mean elevation per cell from all 1,377 `height_x-y.raw` f32 grids (range
  −5.59..18.17, median −0.98): the single great massif fills
  **y ≈ 0–13, x ≈ −2..+12** — the TOP-LEFT (small-y) corner — with a
  secondary ridge east at y ≈ 10–20; everything y ≳ 21 is low.
- The archipelago layer (`layer_island.png`) paints its islands in a band
  at mid-to-large y; the two tile-space island clusters sit at large y
  (x 41–43 / y 28–31).
- Placeholder-identical albedo cells (276 sharing hash `0ff430e9` — this
  also explains the spec's "273⊂276 subset" residue) fill the bottom band,
  i.e. the un-authored open sea is at LARGE y.

Small-y = mountains = "Northern Kingdom"; large-y = lowlands + southern
sea + archipelago. **North = small y**, east = +x. Every directional text
fits with zero contrivance; under the compass-literal alternative every one
of them would need to be upside-down.
