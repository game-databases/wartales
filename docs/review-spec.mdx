## Findings

**F1 — MAJOR · citation overclaim: locales not "extracted & adler-MATCHed".**
`spec.md:53`, `locales.source-per-locale.all` comment: "res.pak:/lang/* ships
all 9 in-client ✅ extracted & adler-MATCHed (TOOL §1)". TOOL §1's extraction
proofs are exactly four files (sealords PNG, Bag PNG→DDS, lightProbe envs.dds,
albedo dds) plus `data.cdb`; `_verify/` contains no lang artifact and
`run-log.txt` mentions none. Presence ×17 files and sizes are TSV/recon-solid,
but the claimed verification event did not happen as cited.
**Fix:** either run the extract+adler32 pass over the 17 lang entries with
wtpak.py before freeze (minutes) and keep the ✅, or restate honestly:
"present ×17 in TSV, sizes cross-checked recon §4; byte-extract pending P1".

**F2 — MAJOR · .fbx counts contradicted by the primary TSVs.**
`spec.md:359-360` (content-policy-holes): "6,946 .fbx=HMD in assets.pak alone
(TOOL §1), 7,204 / 2.13 GB pack-wide (recon §7)". The complete entry TSVs give
assets.pak **5,888** .fbx (1.92 GB) + content.pak **217** (31.6 MB) =
**6,105 ≈ 1.96 GB pack-wide**. The two named sources disagree with each other
and with the entry listings this same spec treats as ground truth; the
MEDIA-CATALOGUE row inherits whatever number freezes.
**Fix:** re-derive from scratch TSVs (cite them directly), correct the line,
and note the discrepancy back to TOOL §1 / recon §7 owners so the source docs
get fixed too.

**F3 — MAJOR · localization filler policy undeclared (canon gap).**
`spec.md:42-56` locales block has no filler-policy declaration.
localization-architecture §5.5 requires each pack to declare its policy
(omit the page vs localized "not yet translated" filler) in its spec; §5.2
also wants game-side codes ↔ BCP-47 mapping recorded, §5.4 makes
`extracted/relinks/locale_availability.jsonl` the driver artifact. Near-moot
for the 9 shipped locales today, load-bearing the moment the ja
authored-translation lane or a future DLC locale ships.
**Fix:** add three lines under `source-per-locale`: filler policy chosen
(either is defensible when coverage is total), client-code mapping
(`zh` client → zh-Hans store naming), and the availability-jsonl obligation.
