## Observations (F1 extracts exact values later)

**Rarity/tier coding.** The inventory does not shout tier: item slots carry
thin warm-grey borders on near-black cells, and higher-quality items read
through a **slot background tint** — olive/gold wash on the equipped helm
(`shot-03`), violet wash and steel-blue wash on rarer goods (`shot-02`),
against neutral dark cells for commons. Count numbers are cream; currency
totals are gold. Combat adds a hard ally/enemy pair — pale-blue movement
tiles vs red threat tiles (`shot-01`) — and the strategic map legend codes
Liberated green vs Adversary red (`shot-05`). Violet appears as a
deliberate secondary accent in arena betting cards (`shot-15`).

**Typography.** Medieval serif everywhere, no clean sans in sight: panel
titles ("Inventory", "Equipment", "Attributes") are a spaced serif in warm
parchment tone; dialogue body is an old-style serif (Garamond/Caslon feel)
on near-black translucent panels with a thin double border (`shot-02`
zoom); attribute labels are serif while their values are the same serif in
green/gold/white. The logo lockup (`shot-19`) is a heavier display serif
with flared terminals — the natural `--font-flavor` anchor; a quiet serif
or humanist sans can carry `--font-ui` if legibility demands, but the
game's own voice is serif-first.

**Surfaces.** Panels are layered near-black browns (quilted-diamond
texture on the inventory frame), 1px warm-grey borders, gold reserved for
currency/selection/AP pips. Empty inventory cells are quiet dark
rectangles — the game itself uses honest empty wells.

## Gaps and failures (recorded honestly)

- **wartales.com unreachable** — connection refused on both attempts
  (browser UA, then crawler UA per owner Note#6). Its media, if any, is
  not in this set.
- **Shiro Games press assets** — shirogames.com carries no Wartales
  screenshot gallery; the four Steam-clan images on the game page are
  other Shiro titles (Frostrail, Space Craft, Farever) and were discarded.
- **Not UI**: most base-game store shots are atmosphere/cinematic frames;
  UI coverage was recovered from DLC store pages (Fief, Tavern, Pits,
  Pirates, Skelmar, Rigel, Beast Hunt, Fires in the Capital).
- **No tooltip close-up at native size** — `shot-06` carries a tooltip but
  at gameplay scale; a dedicated hi-res tooltip crop may need the client
  corpus later.
- **Steam Summer Sale banner** in the news feed is Valve-template art —
  not game identity, excluded.
